# Sponsor KEEBOX LIST™

KEEBOX LIST™ is visited by developers looking for tools, libraries and learning resources — exactly when they are choosing what to use. Sponsoring KEEBOX puts your brand in front of them while keeping the project free for everyone.

## Why sponsor

- **The right audience** — developers actively searching for tools in AI, cloud, data, web, mobile, DevOps and more.
- **Context, not noise** — your logo appears on the topic pages that match your product (for example, a hosting company in *Cloud & Free Tiers*).
- **Good for your brand** — you are visibly supporting a free, ad-free, open-source project.
