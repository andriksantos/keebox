# Credits & Licenses

KEEBOX LIST™ exists thanks to thousands of maintainers and contributors who curate open lists of developer resources. This page explains how we use their work and credits every list we rely on.

## How KEEBOX uses other lists

| License of the original list | What KEEBOX does |
| --- | --- |
| **Public domain** (CC0, Unlicense) | Imported into the [KEEBOX Library](library/index.md) — 444 lists — with a link to the source on every page. |
| **Requires attribution** (MIT, CC BY, CC BY-SA, Apache, GPL…) | Not copied. Credited and linked below — 109 lists. |
| **No license or restrictive** (all rights reserved, non-commercial, no-derivatives) | Not reproduced — 78 lists. At most linked from the [Awesome Lists Directory](lists.md). |

Every curated page written by KEEBOX (AI, Python, Go, Cloud, etc.) links directly to each project's official repository.
